# Privacy Policy for Netflix No Autoplay Banner

**Last updated: May 18, 2026**

## Overview

Netflix No Autoplay Banner is a Chrome extension that pauses and mutes the autoplay video banner on the Netflix homepage. It does nothing else.

## Data Collection

This extension does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any user data of any kind. This includes:

- No browsing history
- No personal information
- No usage analytics
- No crash reports
- No communication with any external server

## Local Storage

The only data stored is your on/off toggle preference, saved locally on your device using Chrome's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API. This data never leaves your browser and is not accessible to anyone other than you.

## Third Parties

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics tools, or remote code.

## Contact

If you have any questions, open an issue at [github.com/angelayanpan/netflix-no-autoplay](https://github.com/angelayanpan/netflix-no-autoplay).
